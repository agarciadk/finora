# visual-identity-refresh — Visual Identity Refresh

## Summary

Groups three purely visual/stylistic inbox ideas that together amount to a restyling initiative: a new color/typography/background direction, page-transition animations, and more colorful account cards. None of these have a defined design direction yet.

## Tasks

- [ ] FIN-036 — Refresh color palette, typography and background
- [ ] FIN-037 — Add page/navigation transition animations
- [ ] FIN-038 — Redesign account cards with more color

## Notes

- All three tasks stay in `refinement`: the inbox only states the *wish* for a visual change, with no palette, font, or animation direction defined anywhere in `.ai-context/`. Implementing any of them today would mean inventing product/design decisions, which is explicitly out of scope for refinement.
- FIN-038 is a more concrete special case of FIN-036 (it's about a specific component), kept as its own task since account cards are called out separately in the inbox and can be iterated on independently of the global palette change.

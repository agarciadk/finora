# FIN-006 — Reorganize profile access and quick actions in the navigation

## Summary

Merges two related inbox ideas that describe the same navigation redesign from two angles: "take the profile out of Settings and move it to the top-right corner" and "move the language/theme/logout actions into the sidebar, above Settings". Doing one without the other would leave the navigation inconsistent, so they're treated as a single unit of work.

## User Story

As a user,
I want quick access to my profile and to language/theme/logout controls without going into Settings,
so that these frequent actions are faster to reach.
